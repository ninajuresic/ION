# ION: Planetary Token

An interactive story site that explains ION, a financial instrument built on the Nature Banking principle, in seven full-screen slides.

**Live site:** [ninajuresic.github.io/ION](https://ninajuresic.github.io/ION/)

## The challenge

ION asks people to rethink where their money goes, which is an abstract idea for most of them. The site walks the visitor through it one step at a time: the problem, the answer, how ION compares to a bank account, and who it suits.

## How it works

**One idea per screen.** Seven full-screen slides replace scrolling: splash, how it works, the problem, the answer, ION vs. a bank account, natural use, and a closing summary. A fixed label at the top shows which section you're on.

**Every way to move forward.** Visitors advance by clicking or tapping anywhere, scrolling, using the arrow keys, swiping on touch screens, or using the navigation dots on desktop.

**Sound and motion that carry the theme.** An animated 3D wave runs along the bottom of every slide. With sound on, it moves to the frequencies of the soundtrack. With sound off, it plays slow standing-wave patterns. A sound button appears shortly after the site loads, and slides fade between each other.

## How I built it

Designed and built in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with the wave drawn on a canvas and driven by the audio's frequency bands.
